# AI Displacement Explorer

A portfolio dashboard presenting how AI adoption is reshaping workforces across countries and industry sectors, built on a synthetic, research-calibrated dataset.

## Language

**Displacement**:
The gross share of a sector's workforce whose roles were lost to AI in a quarter. Always shown alongside Net change.
_Avoid_: Job loss, layoffs, net displacement

**Net change**:
Displacement offset by the new roles AI created in the same sector and quarter; negative means more roles lost than created.
_Avoid_: Net displacement, net loss

**Replacement rate**:
The number of new roles AI created for every 100 roles displaced, over the same countries, Sectors and period.
_Avoid_: Recovery rate, roles regained, replacement ratio

**Sector**:
One of the ten industry groupings the dataset reports on.
_Avoid_: Industry, vertical

**Headline finding**:
A single conclusion drawn from the data and presented above the Explorer with its supporting chart.
_Avoid_: Insight, story, takeaway

**Explorer**:
The free-form part of the dashboard where a visitor filters by country, Sector and time to chart any metric.
_Avoid_: Dashboard (that is the whole site), tool

**Selection**:
The single shared choice of metric, Sector, quarter, country and pinned countries that drives every view in the Explorer.
_Avoid_: Filters, state, query

**Synthetic data**:
Simulated figures calibrated to published research rather than measured observations; the dashboard labels all its numbers as such.
_Avoid_: Real data, estimates, projections
